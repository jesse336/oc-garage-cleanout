What We Don't Take

A few things we can't haul.

For safety and environmental reasons, these items require specialized disposal.

  • Hazardous materials
  • Paint or chemicals
  • Electronics and e-waste
  • Construction debris
  • Appliances containing Freon (AC units, refrigerators)
